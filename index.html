Honey Traceability System

A software concept for tracking honey from the beehive to the consumer. The system brings together beekeeper and hive information, quality verification details, and QR-code-based product traceability so customers can learn more about the origin and journey of the honey they purchase.

Key focus areas

Beekeeper & Hive RecordsQuality VerificationQR-Based TraceabilityHive-to-Consumer Tracking

Focus: Transparency, product authenticity, and trust in the honey supply chain.

Smart Grocery Expiry & Discount Management

A software solution to help grocery stores reduce product wastage and avoid losses caused by items reaching their expiry dates before being sold. The system tracks product expiry dates and alerts store managers when items are nearing expiry, so they can put those products on sale in time.

Key focus areas

Expiry-Date TrackingNear-Expiry AlertsDiscount / Sale RemindersInventory ManagementFood Waste Reduction

Focus: Reducing avoidable food waste while helping store owners minimise inventory losses.
